in on at, listen to…

▼

Место: in (в), on (на поверхности), under (под), next to (рядом), between (между), behind (за), in front of (перед).

Время: at 5 o'clock, on Monday, in July, in 2020. at night, in the morning.

Устойчивые: listen to, look at, wait for, depend on, interested in, good at, afraid of.

Ошибки: in the weekend → at/on the weekend; listen music → listen to music.
