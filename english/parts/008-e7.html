have / has и there is/are

15 вопросов

▼

have / has — «иметь». I/you/we/they have; he/she/it has.

I have a dog. She has a cat. Do you have a bike? He does not (doesn't) have a car.

There is / there are — «есть, имеется»: There is a book on the table. There are two chairs.

have got (британский вариант): I've got a pen = I have a pen.

На ОГЭ часто: choose have/has или there is/there are по числу и лицу.
