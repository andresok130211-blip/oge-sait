Прилагательные: big, small, new, old, good, bad, happy, sad, interesting, boring, easy, difficult.

Порядок: a big red bus (оценка/размер → цвет → существительное) — на ОГЭ достаточно простых цепочек.

Степени: tall–taller–the tallest; interesting–more interesting–the most interesting.

good–better–best, bad–worse–worst, far–farther/further–farthest.

as … as: as tall as. not as … as: not as big as.
